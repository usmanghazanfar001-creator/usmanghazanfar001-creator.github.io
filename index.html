YT Comment Helper

YT Comment Helper is a small personal tool that helps the owner of a YouTube channel manage comments on their own channel.

What it does

  • Reads recent, unanswered comments on the channel owner's own videos.
  • Drafts short replies for the channel owner to review.
  • Posts a reply to the channel owner's own channel comments.

It is not a public service. It is used only by its developer, on their own YouTube channel, and it does not collect data from any other users.
